# Execution Plan

## Delivery philosophy

Full product vision, incremental release. Build one vertical slice at a
time. Do not begin the next checkpoint until acceptance and approval of
the current one.

## Workstreams

1.  Product and UX foundation
2.  Engineering platform and identity
3.  Marketplace and restaurant catalog
4.  Cart, pricing, payments and order lifecycle
5.  Delivery logistics
6.  Independent inspection and trust engine
7.  Support, reviews and complaints
8.  Admin, finance and analytics
9.  AI and advanced integrations
10. Security, compliance and Kolkata pilot

## Release strategy

-   Internal development
-   Staging environment
-   Closed restaurant/inspector beta
-   Limited Kolkata pilot
-   Public zone-by-zone rollout
-   City expansion only after operational metrics and safety governance
    are stable

## Definition of done

Acceptance criteria met; tests pass; docs updated; security review
completed for sensitive changes; no unresolved critical defect; staging
verified; founder approves push/deploy; release notes prepared.

See `docs/CHECKPOINTS/CHECKPOINT_INDEX.md` for granular sequence.
